# Bank Customer Segmentation | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing banking customers, understanding their transaction behaviour, identifying valuable customer segments, and evaluating customer profitability and risk.

An interactive Power BI report was developed to transform customer and transaction data into meaningful business insights. It provides a consolidated view of customer demographics, spending patterns, customer loyalty, revenue contribution, and risk indicators to support data-driven banking decisions.

## Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Customer Demographics Analysis

Examines the customer base through demographic and geographical characteristics.

- Analyzes the total customer population and average customer age.
- Compares male and female customer distribution.
- Identifies customer concentration across different geographical locations.
- Examines age-group distribution and gender patterns across age categories.
- Provides a detailed customer-level view for demographic exploration.

**Business Insights:** Helps understand the composition of the bank's customer base, identify dominant customer demographics, recognize geographical customer concentrations, and identify potential target groups for personalized banking services.

### 2. Transaction Behaviour Insights

Explores customer transaction patterns, transaction values, and activity over time.

- Evaluates total transaction volume, average transaction amount, and highest transaction value.
- Analyzes transaction trends over time.
- Examines transaction activity across different hours of the day.
- Compares transaction amounts across different customer age groups.
- Provides detailed transaction-level analysis with interactive filters.

**Business Insights:** Helps identify transaction patterns, understand customer spending behaviour, recognize peak transaction periods, and explore differences in transaction activity across customer groups.

### 3. Customer Segmentation Analysis

Categorizes customers into Loyal, New, and Lost segments using RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis.

- Evaluates the distribution of customers across different segments.
- Identifies loyal and lost customers.
- Analyzes revenue generated from new customers.
- Compares transaction frequency and revenue contribution across segments.
- Examines segment distribution across age groups and gender.
- Uses RFM scores to explore customer purchasing behaviour.

**Business Insights:** Helps identify valuable and loyal customers, understand customer retention patterns, recognize customers who may require re-engagement, and evaluate revenue contributions across different customer segments.

### 4. Profitability & Risk Indicators

Evaluates customer value, creditworthiness, and associated risk factors.

- Analyzes average customer credit score and total monetary value.
- Identifies high-risk customers.
- Examines credit score distribution across customer segments.
- Evaluates customer distribution across different risk levels.
- Explores the relationship between account balance and risk level.
- Identifies high-revenue but high-risk customers.
- Compares customer value scores across segments.

**Business Insights:** Helps assess customer profitability alongside potential financial risk, identify high-value customers requiring closer monitoring, and understand how customer value and credit risk vary across segments.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI:** Interactive dashboards, data visualization, and reporting.
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX:** Calculated measures, business metrics, and customer segmentation analysis.

## Key Project Highlights

- Four analytical dashboard sections covering different aspects of banking customer analysis.
- Interactive slicers and filters for exploring customer and transaction data.
- KPI cards, bar charts, column charts, donut charts, treemaps, scatter plots, funnel charts, and detailed tables.
- Customer segmentation using RFM analysis.
- Business-focused reporting with an emphasis on customer value, revenue, and risk assessment.
- Multi-page report navigation for a structured analytical experience.
